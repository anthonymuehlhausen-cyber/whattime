Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Atlanta in?

Atlanta is in the America/New_York time zone (EST & EDT). Its standard-time offset is UTC-05:00 from UTC.

Does Atlanta observe daylight saving time?

Yes — Atlanta observes daylight saving time. Clocks move forward to EDT (UTC-04:00) in spring and back to EST (UTC-05:00) in autumn. The exact 2026 change dates are in the table on this page.

What is the current UTC offset in Atlanta?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Atlanta's current offset — it is UTC-05:00 on standard time and UTC-04:00 during daylight saving.
