Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Phoenix in?

Phoenix is in the America/Phoenix time zone (MST). Its standard-time offset is UTC-07:00 from UTC.

Does Phoenix observe daylight saving time?

No — Phoenix stays on MST (UTC-07:00) all year and does not observe daylight saving time. That is why its time difference with cities that do change clocks shifts twice a year.

What is the current UTC offset in Phoenix?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Phoenix's current offset — it is UTC-07:00 on standard time.
