Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This Privacy Policy explains how WhatTime ("we", "the site") handles information when you use this website. The site is operated by Sideline HQ.

What we collect

WhatTime has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms that ask for personal information. All clocks and conversions are computed in your own browser — the times you look up are never sent to us. Your browser's own time zone is read locally, on your device, only to display your local clock.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads, which keeps the site free. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com. We also store one functional cookie/local-storage flag ("wt-consent") that simply remembers you dismissed the cookie notice.

Analytics and logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) for security and operations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
