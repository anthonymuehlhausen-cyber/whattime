Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Dublin in?

Dublin is in the Europe/Dublin time zone (GMT & IST). Its standard-time offset is UTC+00:00 from UTC.

Does Dublin observe daylight saving time?

Yes — Dublin observes daylight saving time. Clocks move forward to IST (UTC+01:00) in spring and back to GMT (UTC+00:00) in autumn. The exact 2026 change dates are in the table on this page.

What is the current UTC offset in Dublin?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Dublin's current offset — it is UTC+00:00 on standard time and UTC+01:00 during daylight saving.
