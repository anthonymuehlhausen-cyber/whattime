Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Singapore in?

Singapore is in the Asia/Singapore time zone (+08). Its standard-time offset is UTC+08:00 from UTC.

Does Singapore observe daylight saving time?

No — Singapore stays on +08 (UTC+08:00) all year and does not observe daylight saving time. That is why its time difference with cities that do change clocks shifts twice a year.

What is the current UTC offset in Singapore?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Singapore's current offset — it is UTC+08:00 on standard time.
