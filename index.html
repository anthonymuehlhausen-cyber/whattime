Frequently asked questions

What time is it right now?

Your local time is shown at the top of this page, read straight from your device's own clock and time zone. The grid below shows the same moment in 24 major world cities, updated every second.

Why is the time difference between two cities different in summer and winter?

Because of daylight saving time — and because cities change clocks on different dates, or not at all. WhatTime computes every time from the IANA time zone database, so differences are always the true ones for today's date.

Does WhatTime work for future dates, like planning a call next month?

Yes — the converter accepts any date and applies the time zone rules in force on that date, including clock changes that happen between now and then.

Which cities do not change their clocks?

Phoenix, Honolulu, Tokyo, Dubai, Singapore, Mumbai and many others stay on one offset all year. Each city page states its daylight saving status and its next clock change, if it has one.
