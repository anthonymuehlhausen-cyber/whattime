Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Hong Kong in?

Hong Kong is in the Asia/Hong_Kong time zone (HKT). Its standard-time offset is UTC+08:00 from UTC.

Does Hong Kong observe daylight saving time?

No — Hong Kong stays on HKT (UTC+08:00) all year and does not observe daylight saving time. That is why its time difference with cities that do change clocks shifts twice a year.

What is the current UTC offset in Hong Kong?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Hong Kong's current offset — it is UTC+08:00 on standard time.
