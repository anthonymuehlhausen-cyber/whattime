Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Auckland in?

Auckland is in the Pacific/Auckland time zone (NZST & NZDT). Its standard-time offset is UTC+12:00 from UTC.

Does Auckland observe daylight saving time?

Yes — Auckland observes daylight saving time. Clocks move forward to NZDT (UTC+13:00) in spring and back to NZST (UTC+12:00) in autumn. The exact 2026 change dates are in the table on this page.

What is the current UTC offset in Auckland?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Auckland's current offset — it is UTC+12:00 on standard time and UTC+13:00 during daylight saving.
