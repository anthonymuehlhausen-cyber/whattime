Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Dubai in?

Dubai is in the Asia/Dubai time zone (+04). Its standard-time offset is UTC+04:00 from UTC.

Does Dubai observe daylight saving time?

No — Dubai stays on +04 (UTC+04:00) all year and does not observe daylight saving time. That is why its time difference with cities that do change clocks shifts twice a year.

What is the current UTC offset in Dubai?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Dubai's current offset — it is UTC+04:00 on standard time.
