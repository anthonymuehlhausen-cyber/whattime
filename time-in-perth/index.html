Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Perth in?

Perth is in the Australia/Perth time zone (AWST). Its standard-time offset is UTC+08:00 from UTC.

Does Perth observe daylight saving time?

No — Perth stays on AWST (UTC+08:00) all year and does not observe daylight saving time. That is why its time difference with cities that do change clocks shifts twice a year.

What is the current UTC offset in Perth?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Perth's current offset — it is UTC+08:00 on standard time.
