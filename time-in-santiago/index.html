Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Santiago in?

Santiago is in the America/Santiago time zone (-04 & -03). Its standard-time offset is UTC-04:00 from UTC.

Does Santiago observe daylight saving time?

Yes — Santiago observes daylight saving time. Clocks move forward to -03 (UTC-03:00) in spring and back to -04 (UTC-04:00) in autumn. The exact 2026 change dates are in the table on this page.

What is the current UTC offset in Santiago?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Santiago's current offset — it is UTC-04:00 on standard time and UTC-03:00 during daylight saving.
