Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Melbourne in?

Melbourne is in the Australia/Melbourne time zone (AEST & AEDT). Its standard-time offset is UTC+10:00 from UTC.

Does Melbourne observe daylight saving time?

Yes — Melbourne observes daylight saving time. Clocks move forward to AEDT (UTC+11:00) in spring and back to AEST (UTC+10:00) in autumn. The exact 2026 change dates are in the table on this page.

What is the current UTC offset in Melbourne?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Melbourne's current offset — it is UTC+10:00 on standard time and UTC+11:00 during daylight saving.
