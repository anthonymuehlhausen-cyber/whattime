UK Clock Changes 2026: BST Start and End Dates

The UK clocks go forward one hour on Sunday, March 29, 2026 at 1:00 AM, marking the start of British Summer Time (BST, UTC+1). They go back on Sunday, October 25, 2026 at 2:00 AM, returning to Greenwich Mean Time (GMT, UTC+0).

UK and Europe change together

YearClocks forward (BST/CEST begins)Clocks back (GMT/CET resumes)
2026Sunday, March 29Sunday, October 25
2027Sunday, March 28Sunday, October 31

The whole of the European Union changes on the same nights as the UK — the last Sundays of March and October. That is two to three weeks after the United States springs forward, which is why the London–New York gap narrows from 5 hours to 4 for part of March — and narrows to 4 again for the week in autumn between the UK change (last Sunday of October) and the US change (first Sunday of November). GMT vs BST

London's clock on this site shows GMT in winter and BST in summer automatically. GMT is also the ancestor of UTC — see UTC vs GMT for the (small) difference.

Frequently asked questions

When do the clocks go forward in the UK in 2026?

Sunday, March 29, 2026 at 1:00 AM — the start of British Summer Time.

When do the clocks go back in the UK in 2026?

Sunday, October 25, 2026 at 2:00 AM — back to Greenwich Mean Time.

Is the UK on GMT or BST right now?

BST (UTC+1) from the last Sunday of March to the last Sunday of October, and GMT (UTC+0) the rest of the year. The live London clock on this site always shows the current one.
