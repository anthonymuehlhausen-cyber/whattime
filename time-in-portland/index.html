Frequently asked questions

What time zone is Portland in?

Portland is in the America/Los_Angeles time zone (PST & PDT). Its standard-time offset is UTC-08:00 from UTC.

Does Portland observe daylight saving time?

Yes — Portland observes daylight saving time. Clocks move forward to PDT (UTC-07:00) in spring and back to PST (UTC-08:00) in autumn. The exact 2026 change dates are in the table on this page.

What is the current UTC offset in Portland?

The live clock at the top of this page shows Portland's current offset — it is UTC-08:00 on standard time and UTC-07:00 during daylight saving.
